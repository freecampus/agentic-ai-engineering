Four numbered milestones

Milestones extend unit challenges rather than adding four more challenges to the course total. Allow a provisional 6–12 hours for each refinement, subject to piloting.

1. Ship a Transparent Research Assistant

Build a framework-free assistant with two read-only tools, typed state, citations, budgets, tests, traces, and a deterministic baseline. Submit an evaluation showing whether agentic control adds value.

Preview Unit 4. Preserve a reproducible environment, baseline, tests, traces, measured trade-offs, and limitations.

2. Ship a Grounded Knowledge Worker

Extend the research assistant with evaluated hybrid retrieval, bounded memory, provenance, and user-controlled deletion. Show that memory improves a longitudinal task without harming privacy or contradiction handling.

Preview Unit 8. Preserve a reproducible environment, baseline, tests, traces, measured trade-offs, and limitations.

3. Deliver a Defended Agent

Take the grounded knowledge worker through evaluation, observability, threat modeling, red-team testing, and human-control review. Repair the highest-risk failure and demonstrate the regression gate.

Preview Unit 16. Preserve a reproducible environment, baseline, tests, traces, measured trade-offs, and limitations.

4. Present a Production Candidate

Deliver an open-source-first agent service with durable execution, local-model support, observability, security controls, CI gates, SLOs, cost model, and rollback. Defend why each autonomous decision remains necessary.

Preview Unit 24. Preserve a reproducible environment, baseline, tests, traces, measured trade-offs, and limitations.

Architecture and advanced checkpoints

Unit 12 asks you to defend a single- or multi-agent design; Unit 20 asks you to build a durable specialist. These are additional integration checkpoints, not numbered milestone projects. Cumulative reviews follow Units 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24.
